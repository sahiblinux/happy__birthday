The card

Dear Noorie,

Birthdays are a decent excuse to say the things I mean all year and never get round to saying.

So — I hope this one's gentler than the last. I hope you do the thing you keep talking about doing. I hope your coffee is always the right temperature and your trains are always on time, and I hope the year hands you a few genuinely good surprises.

And I hope you know that nobody is waiting for you to earn the good stuff. You already did.

Happy birthday.

— with love

P.S. There's cake in the fridge. I checked.
